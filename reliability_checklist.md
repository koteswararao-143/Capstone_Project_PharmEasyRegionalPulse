# Reliability Checklist

The report is not marked approved until these four workflow steps are complete.

## 1. Safety check

I checked that the memo and narrative use regional aggregate metrics only and
do not expose personally identifiable customer information. 

## 2. Validation

I checked that the narrative numbers come from the Part 2 SQL-backed monthly
metrics and that the Guntur April → May percentage is +122.19%. 

## 3. Critique / refine

I checked that the recommendation does not present an unsupported external
cause as fact; possible explanations remain hypotheses until additional data
supports them. 

## 4. Human sign-off

I checked that the review gate requires an explicit 'approve', 'edit', or
'reject' decision and records the reviewer note, run ID, region, decision, and
timestamp in the audit log.
